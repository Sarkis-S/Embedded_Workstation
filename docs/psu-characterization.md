### CV-to-CC Transition

With a 100 Ω load connected, the current limit was reduced until the
power supply transitioned from constant-voltage (CV) operation to
constant-current (CC) operation.

The transition was recorded to demonstrate the supply automatically
reducing its output voltage to maintain the configured current limit.

CC test:<br>
Current limit ≈ 20 mA<br>
