# Build History

## Rev A — Initial Workstation

The workstation began as a compact acrylic platform for organizing
embedded-development hardware and breadboard experiments.

The design evolved during construction rather than from a finished
mechanical specification.

Major features added during the initial build included:

- Two acrylic layers separated by standoffs
- Arduino and ESP32 development areas
- Breadboard prototyping area
- Logic analyzer storage/access
- Cable management beneath the upper deck
- Space for external instrumentation
- Provision for external bench power

The acrylic mounting holes were drilled after physically arranging the
hardware and evaluating component placement.

## Power System Development

The workstation was originally used primarily with USB and development
board power.

A WANPTEK DPS3010U bench power supply was later introduced to support
controlled external power for loads such as motors, fans, drivers, and
higher-current experiments.

This led to development of a dedicated workstation power-interface
module.
